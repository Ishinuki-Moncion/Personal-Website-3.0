## Blade Runner 2049 (Territory Studio / Deakins / Villeneuve)

- **[VERIFIED]** Villeneuve's brief: imagine a world where digital technology
  no longer exists; conceive tech that is *physical, organic, grounded*, with
  interface design reflecting each character's status and societal divide.
  — territorystudio.com/project/blade-runner-2049/
- **[VERIFIED]** Low-status interfaces (LAPD, K's spinner) age vocabulary:
  **warping, ghosting, colour degradation, glitches, surface textures**. — ibid.
- **[VERIFIED]** Wallace Corp (wealth/power): **pure, minimal, geometric
  black-and-white** interfaces, deliberately contrasted with degraded tech. — ibid.
- **[VERIFIED]** K's apartment: severe prefab, plastic feel, references drawn
  from **Japan and Hong Kong** — minimal/synthetic as character coding. — theasc.com
- **[VERIFIED]** "Environment as a character": Beijing-in-smog reference, cold
  L.A. with rain/snow exteriors, stark Brutalism (South Bank, Barbican,
  Trellick Tower). — theasc.com
- **[VERIFIED]** Vegas orange was made **in-camera with Lee gels** (790
  Moroccan Pink + 105 Orange; Golden Amber up high so light reads lighter
  aloft) — warmth belongs to the light source, not the grade. — theasc.com
- **[VERIFIED — the governing hologram finding]** For Joi, Deakins **argued
  for minimal artifacting**: transparency visible only against highlights and
  subtle even then; VFX tests adding pixelation/image distressing were
  **rejected as "too fussy"** in favor of overlapping performances shot in the
  same real light. Expensive hologram = restraint + matched lighting, NOT
  heavy glitch. — theasc.com
- **[VERIFIED]** Territory was embedded in the art department from early
  production: screens are production design/narrative, not decoration. — vimeo.com/240920045
- **[EXTRACTED]** The Pink Joi ad was played at true scale on a 40×30-ft LED
  through mist so **the hologram itself was the scene's light source** —
  holograms light their world. — theasc.com
- **[EXTRACTED]** Brief in four words: "abstract, organic, optical, physical";
  motivated light everywhere (holographic Elvis, fake sun off water). — vfxblog.com

## Ghost in the Shell (2017 + 1995 heritage)

- **[EXTRACTED]** "Sologram" = Rupert Sanders' coinage for **solid holograms**:
  "a particle system of light that can be moved and augmented in Z-space" —
  the city's neon painted as volumetric light. — 80.lv (Ash Thorp)
- **[EXTRACTED]** The signature artifact is **voxelization/particulation** —
  captured performances converted to volumetric particles — NOT scanlines or
  flicker. Built from an 80-camera rig, ~24 volumetric scans/sec. — artofvfx.com, vfxvoice.com
- **[EXTRACTED]** Solograms receive **environmental light** (almost-solid
  objects lit by their surroundings), unlike self-glowing billboards. — artofvfx.com
- **[EXTRACTED]** Every city sologram was **individually art-directed per
  shot** (hundreds, placed deliberately — never procedural scatter). — artofvfx.com
- **[EXTRACTED]** Territory was briefed to **avoid flat screens entirely** —
  all tech exists volumetrically in Z-space; they built a **"hologlobe"**, a
  holographic globe device generating people/objects in holo-space — a direct
  filmic precedent for this site's data-globe. — territorystudio.com/project/ghost-in-the-shell/

## Metal Gear Solid (tactical instruments)

- **[DATA]** MGSV UI hue discipline: 68 of 122 dominant swatches across 53
  catalogued screens sit in **hue 193–201** (cyan-blue band). Recurring iDroid
  panel blue **#0f394c / #0f394d** (H≈199–200, S≈80%, B≈30%). — gameuidatabase.com
- **[DATA]** Fan iDroid recreation pairing: field **#113039**, active/highlight
  **#b2f5fd** (dark blue-green field + near-white cyan foreground — not
  saturated neon). — github.com/TylerJDev/iDroid
- **[DATA]** Soliton radar state tints (fan CSS recreation, one tint per state
  across the whole panel): Jamming **#028944**, Evasion **#7B6F12**, Alert
  **#BD2B25**, on a dark teal→black gradient (#062829→#0a0a0a). — codepen.io
- **[EXTRACTED]** MGS1's entire HUD = **five instruments** (vitals, items,
  clock, radar, reticle) — extreme information restraint. iDroid map has two
  modes: satellite imagery and a **Soliton-schematic mode** — the radar
  grammar carried forward. — gameuidatabase.com, metalgear.fandom.com

## Cyberpunk 2077 (scan grammar / rules-driven UI)

- **[EXTRACTED]** UI coherence came from an explicit internal **UI Art Bible /
  rule system** grounded in lore ("rules-driven, not ad hoc"), organizing four
  era styles: Entropism, Kitsch, Neo-Militarism, Neo-Kitsch. — behance (CDPR Senior UI Artist)
- **[EXTRACTED]** **Red encodes the hostile/military layer** — a deliberate
  inversion of red-as-error; the artist calls calibrating aggression-in-red
  the hardest part. Alert red must stay semantically loaded. — behance
- **[EXTRACTED]** Shipped UI typeface: **Rajdhani** (free Google Font; CDPR
  built a Cyrillic extension for the game). Concrete, freely usable HUD face. — opacity.ru

## Cross-cutting synthesis (drives design-language v2)

1. **Restraint is the price of "expensive."** The most-cited holograms in the
   canon minimize artifacts. Glitch grammar exists (BR2049 uses warping/
   ghosting) but it encodes AGE AND LOW STATUS — Tokyo's own instruments on
   this site are high-status and should artifact rarely and subtly.
2. **The artifact of the future is particulation, not scanlines.** GITS
   solograms are particle systems in Z-space; our globe is already a particle
   system — build-ins/acquisitions should assemble from particles.
3. **Holograms are light sources.** They illuminate their surroundings (Pink
   Joi); glow-casting from holo elements is canon-correct.
4. **Warmth lives in the light source, not the grade** (Lee-gel Vegas): amber
   should come from emitters (city lights, signal events), never a wash.
5. **Status hierarchy through fidelity:** minimal-geometric = power/instrument;
   warped/ghosted = age/world texture. Two registers, never blended.
6. **Tactical palette anchor:** the iDroid family (#0f394c field, #b2f5fd
   active, hue 193–201 discipline) is the researched home for instrument
   panels; alert red (#BD2B25 family) only for genuine alert semantics.
7. **Typography:** Rajdhani (free) for HUD numerals/tags; monospace for data
   lines; JP glyphs first-class (M PLUS family already in the stack).
8. **Information restraint:** five-instrument HUDs; per-shot art direction —
   place every element deliberately, never scatter.
