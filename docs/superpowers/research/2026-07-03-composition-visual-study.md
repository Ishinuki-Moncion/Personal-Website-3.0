## Principles
Tier legend: [VERIFIED] cross-checked across ≥3 board refs · [EXTRACTED] read directly off one primary reference · [DATA] measured pixel/ratio value · [WORKING] practitioner judgment/extrapolation, no measurement.

1. [VERIFIED] **Negative space is the primary device, not the leftover.** Reserve the majority of the frame as empty dark field and let one small subject carry it — BR2049's two figures occupy under ~2% of the corridor frame at the vanishing point, 2001 floats its celestial subjects in ~85% black, and the CSM site is empty but for edge micro-nav + one floating image. Cross-checked across film + web. For daikieOS: default every section to generous void; one focal element per viewport, never a filled canvas. (ref: 005-br2049-018, 009-2001-01, 016-brut-csm; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)
2. [VERIFIED] **Focal hierarchy is driven by luminance on a near-black base** — the brightest region wins the eye first, every time. BR2049 concentrates light on the corridor's slats and its far end (005) and on the single sky-gap in the dark ruin (007); 2001 puts the only bright value at the sun-crest (009). Cross-checked ≥3 refs. On the dark site, make the intended first-read the highest-luminance element and hold everything else dim. (ref: 005-br2049-018, 007-br2049-028, 009-2001-01; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)
3. [VERIFIED] **Push chrome and labels to the margins/corners; reserve the center for the subject.** Territory's thermal HUD stacks its data block in the top-left corner with bracket tags at the right edge, leaving the specimen centered (012); CSM parks all navigation at the four edges around an empty middle (016); transmediale rails nav left and meta right around the content (018). Cross-checked across 3 FUI/web refs. Frame content with peripheral data — never overlay it. (ref: 012-terr-fui-m31, 016-brut-csm, 018-brut-transmediale; src: https://territorystudio.com/project/blade-runner-2049/)
4. [EXTRACTED] **One dominant focal point = one break in an otherwise uniform field.** A lone spinner disrupts the radial solar-farm grid (001); a single lit doorway interrupts a wall of foliage (008). The eye lands where the pattern breaks or the light concentrates. Give each composition exactly one such disruptor and let repetition carry the rest. (ref: 001-br2049-002, 008-br2049-034; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)
5. [EXTRACTED] **Build the eye-path with convergence — one-point perspective and receding rhythm lead the gaze to the focus.** The corridor's light-slat rhythm and floor recede to the distant figures (005); 2001's concentric ring corridor funnels to a dead-center vanishing point (010). Use receding rails/repetition as arrows toward the intended first-read. (ref: 005-br2049-018, 010-2001-08; src: https://film-grab.com/2010/07/06/2001-a-space-odyssey/)
6. [EXTRACTED] **Choose a symmetry mode deliberately.** Kubrick centers and mirrors (010 concentric corridor, 009 vertical celestial stack) for order and monumentality; Deakins/Villeneuve pushes the subject off-center against a heavy void (008, 002) for tension. daikieOS default = asymmetric off-center for tension, reserving dead-center symmetry for the globe/hero as the one monument. (ref: 010-2001-08, 009-2001-01, 002-br2049-006; src: https://film-grab.com/2010/07/06/2001-a-space-odyssey/)
7. [EXTRACTED] **Anchor mass to one edge and leave the opposite region open.** BR2049 seats the near-black LAPD monolith along the bottom with hazy void above (002); 032c drops a solid red text-block across the bottom third under an image bed (015). Weight one edge, breathe the rest — never center-fill the mass. (ref: 002-br2049-006, 015-brut-032c; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)
8. [EXTRACTED] **Density is legitimate only when gridded.** The dense industrial ruin reads because catwalks and stairs sit on implied verticals + horizontals (007); Territory packs hundreds of glyphs into three aligned monospace columns (013). A dense block that resolves to a grid is information; one that doesn't is noise. Always seat density on a grid and ring it with breathing room. (ref: 007-br2049-028, 013-terr-fui-d37; src: https://territorystudio.com/project/blade-runner-2049/)
9. [EXTRACTED] **Structure the page with thin-rule cellular framing, like an instrument or ledger.** sfudutch/Philips divides the viewport into labeled cells with hairline rules — a close "X" cell, a header cell, a "Transcription" cell, a full-bleed media cell (017). This is the web cousin of the site's existing HUD corner brackets. Divide layout with 1px rules into *named* cells rather than floating boxes. (ref: 017-brut-sfudutch; src: https://brutalistwebsites.com/)
10. [EXTRACTED] **The Swiss 3-zone grid + exactly one accent color.** transmediale runs a flush-left nav rail, a centered content column, and a right meta margin (emblem, de/en, blue +/−), all keyed to a single blue (018). The site's asymmetric section grids (about 1.1fr/0.9fr, work 7fr/5fr) already follow this skeleton — hold the single-accent (cyan) discipline and let structure, not color, do the work. (ref: 018-brut-transmediale; src: https://brutalistwebsites.com/)
11. [EXTRACTED] **Oversized display type can *be* the composition.** 032c lets a giant headline dominate the frame, demoting the photo to a textured bed and adding just one red accent bar (015). For hero and section headers, let type occupy the focal role at large scale over a dimmed image, single accent only — the words are the subject. (ref: 015-brut-032c; src: https://brutalistwebsites.com/)
12. [EXTRACTED] **Nest the focal element inside a secondary frame (frame-within-frame).** BR2049 sets the distant figure inside a lit doorway/vitrine amid the forest chaos (008); Territory bezels the live face-scan inside a physical monitor with a labeled button-rail (014). An in-scene aperture directs the read and adds depth — use a bracket box, bezel, or aperture around the intended subject. (ref: 008-br2049-034, 014-terr-fui-ljo19; src: https://territorystudio.com/project/blade-runner-2049/)
13. [EXTRACTED] **Repeat a module to build the field, then break it once for the focus.** BR2049's morgue wall of identical drawers (006) and the solar-farm's radial cell-rings (001) turn repetition into texture that a single event punctuates; the site's 3-column gallery-grid is the same device. Modular repetition creates rhythm and communicates scale — the single break is the focal point (see P4). (ref: 006-br2049-023, 001-br2049-002; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)
14. [WORKING] **Inherit the cinematic read via letterbox/aspect discipline.** These frames read as *composed* partly because 2.20–2.35:1 forces horizontal negative space and a low, wide eye-path (005, 002). On the site, frame hero/gallery media as wide, short "letterbox" bands (constrained max-width + generous vertical padding) rather than full-bleed square blocks, to borrow that horizontal breathing room. No measured ratio taken — practitioner judgment. (ref: 005-br2049-018, 002-br2049-006; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)
15. [WORKING] **Measure discipline is compositional breathing room in text.** The site already caps line length (hero-sub 46ch, geo-copy 68ch, row-desc 42ch); keep text columns narrow so paragraphs read as considered blocks floating in space — the textual form of the sparse-subject-in-void principle (P1). transmediale and CSM both hold hard-narrow content columns against wide empty margins. Never let body text fill the viewport width. (ref: 018-brut-transmediale, 016-brut-csm; src: https://brutalistwebsites.com/)
