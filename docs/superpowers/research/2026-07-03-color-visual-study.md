`bladerunnerNNN.jpg`), **Drive** (63 frames, `N (nnn).jpg` — spaces/parens URL-encoded `%20%28…%29`),

indexed posts are Ghibli/TV/misc, **not** the noir/cyberpunk canon; its actual value (frame + extracted
palette-swatch pairs) is **replaced here by generating my own PIL palette reductions + exact-hex samples**
off the film-grab frames — better, because it is my analysis on my chosen frames with real measured values

prior surfaces' learning); a color-script grab would need the og:image/CDN fallback — deferred, film-grab
+ PIL fully carried the surface. **This is the surface where measurement pays off:** a PIL pass
(luminance histograms → % frame in shadow; HSV hue-bucket accent:base ratios; median-cut palettes;
targeted patch-averaged pixel hexes) yields genuine **[DATA]**, upgrading several principles past the
[EXTRACTED] ceiling the globe/rain entries hit. **Gap:** my Drive gallery-position guesses landed mostly
on its *daylight* scenes, so Drive's iconic magenta elevator / cyan night-drive frames went uncaptured

## Principles
Tier legend: [VERIFIED] cross-checked across ≥3 board refs · [EXTRACTED] read directly off one primary reference (hex sampled by eye) · [DATA] measured pixel value (PIL luminance histogram / HSV hue-bucket / patch-averaged sample) · [WORKING] practitioner judgment/extrapolation, no measurement.

1. [DATA] **Put the base at the value floor and hold the highlights down — the image lives in the bottom third of the range.** Measured across the kept night frames, ~80–99% of pixels sit below quarter-grey (L<64/255): LAPD 99.4%, Drive skyline 96.4%, Vegas interior 92.7%, Drive tunnel 92.5%, seawall 89.7%, Joi apt 86.8%, solar farm 86.7% — median ≈88%. Highlights (L>200) measure ≈0% of frame on all of them; the deepest silhouettes read #010101. Grade the site so the base sits below ~L20/255 and true white is spent only on tiny emitters. Never let the darks lift to a comfortable "dark grey UI" — crush them. (ref: 002-lapd, 005-interior, 015-drive-day; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

2. [DATA] **The warm accent is a single-digit percent of the frame — budget it, don't spread it.** On every cool-field frame the amber/red signal occupies ~0–6% of frame area (measured warm-signal: LAPD 0.0%, solar farm 0.0%, seawall 0.0%, Drive skyline 1.0%, Joi apt 6.3% — the high end, and that is her *entire* garment), while the cool field fills 40–99%. Warm:cool area runs from ~1:8 (a hero warm garment) to ~1:60+ (a few pinpoints), and is literally zero on many cool shots. If warm and cool are near-equal area, the noir is gone. Set an accent-area budget of a few percent per view. (ref: 002-lapd, 004-joi-apt; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

3. [VERIFIED] **One temperature per frame — "teal AND orange" is a cut, not an in-frame mix.** Cross-checked: cool shots (001, 002, 013, 015, 018, 019) run teal-dominant with warm essentially absent; warm shots (005, 010, 012, 016) run amber-dominant with cool absent — the measured warm:cool ratio flips from ~0.0 to >7–75 between zones, almost never balancing both hues in one frame. The film's famous complementary palette is achieved *across the edit* by alternating committed single-temperature scenes. Commit each section/state to one temperature; switch temperature at a boundary, don't dapple both into one screen. (ref: 010-vegas-dust, 013-seawall, 005-interior; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

4. [DATA] **A "flooded" atmospheric frame is one hue across a narrow value band, not full contrast.** The Vegas orange frame samples #d27705 (sky) → #7e2103 (ground) → #771c02 (figure): a single hue (~25–30°) with *no* pixel reaching true black or true white (measured: shadow<L25 = 0.0%, highlight>L200 = 0.0% — the whole frame compressed into a mid-orange band). The drama is the near-absence of contrast — a fog of one color. For an all-over atmospheric look, compress the frame into one hue over a limited value band rather than reaching for range. (ref: 010-vegas-dust; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

5. [EXTRACTED] **Neon is a bloomed core + additive halo on black, never a flat colored shape.** The giant Joi ad reads as a saturated magenta/violet core ([DATA] patch #421846, brighter at center) blooming into a soft violet halo over a #110c13 near-black field, its amber text glowing outward — the sign is a light source that fogs the air around it. Render emitters/neon as a saturated core plus an additive soft halo that lifts the surrounding near-black a few stops *in the source's own hue*; a hard-edged colored rectangle reads as a sticker, not light. (ref: 009-vegas-approach, 004-joi-apt; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

6. [EXTRACTED] **Distributed/city light is sparse warm micro-emitters on near-black — light is an event.** In the LAPD frame the only saturated color is a scatter of red aircraft-warning pinpoints (each a few px, warm halo ≈#463437) on a #1b2b2e teal-black platform; the Drive skyline is a black field speckled with warm sodium points (patch-averaged #190d0b) plus one cool billboard (#4e5753). This is the same "clustered emitters, scarce light" law the globe surface found (globe P1/P2), now confirmed in graded live film. Model background/city light as tiny warm points over near-black, not an even glow. (ref: 002-lapd, 015-drive-day; src: https://film-grab.com/2012/07/01/drive/)

7. [EXTRACTED] **Warm accents are motivated practicals — a bulb, a sign, a garment, skin — not a global tint.** Sapper's green stairwell carries one warm tungsten practical (#655024) in an otherwise cyan-green field; the noodle bar mixes cyan fluorescent (#002e4e) with warm hanging bulbs; Joi's amber garment (#cf766f) is the warm note in a teal spinner. Place warm accents as diegetic point sources so the eye reads them as objects in the world, not a color filter laid over everything. (ref: 003-protein, 019-br1982-noodlebar, 004-joi-apt; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

8. [VERIFIED] **Key subjects warm against a cool field for separation — warm-on-cool does the work an outline would.** Drive's tunnel keys warm skin on the two faces against a desaturated teal fluorescent field (#76805d); the noodle bar warm-keys Deckard against cyan; Joi reads by her warm garment/skin against the teal city. Cross-checked across three films. When a subject or label must read against the dark cool base, key it a few degrees warmer and a touch brighter rather than adding a stroke or a box. (ref: 018-drive-night, 019-br1982-noodlebar, 004-joi-apt; src: https://film-grab.com/2012/07/01/drive/)

9. [DATA] **The cool base is a *stable* teal-cyan across every film and scene — pin it to one token.** Measured dominant cool hexes: solar farm #1a251f/#1a2b2a, LAPD field #1b2b2e, seawall #08080b→#13454c, Drive tunnel #76805d, BR1982 noodle #002e4e, rooftop #264650 — all land in blue-green (teal, hue ~180–200°), never pure blue and never yellow-green. Consistency of the base hue is what fuses separate shots into one graded world. Pin the site's cool base to a single teal-cyan token (≈ hue 185–195°, very low value, e.g. #0c1f22) and reuse it everywhere. (ref: 013-seawall, 019-br1982-noodlebar, 001-la-teal; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

10. [EXTRACTED] **Green is the "life / other" pole — deliberate and rare, never part of the base.** The one saturated-green daylight frame (organic forest, #263815/#49591d) and the sickly fluorescent greens stand apart from the teal/amber spine; documented cinematography uses green for Joi and for Wallace's creations, with subtly *lifted* green shadows rather than a green cast. Reserve green as a rare third accent for "living/other/uncanny" states; if it appears in shadows keep it a faint lift, not a wash. (ref: 008-archive, 003-protein; src: https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/symmetry-color-cinematography-blade-runner/)

11. [DATA] **Legibility comes from luminance + same-hue bloom, not hue-on-hue contrast.** Because a frame commits to one hue, subjects and text separate by being *brighter*, not by a complementary color: the amber Joi text and magenta core are the brightest pixels in 009, the headlamp (#13454c) is the brightest thing in the near-black seawall (013), and measured highlight area is ≈0% except those emitter cores. For HUD/type on the dark base, separate by luminance and a soft glow in the *same* color family — do not drop a complementary block behind text. Keep the palette tight and let brightness carry the read. (ref: 009-vegas-approach, 013-seawall; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

12. [EXTRACTED] **Even the bright/daylight grades stay tinted and low-saturation — there is no neutral-white state.** Drive's golden LA-river floods warm (#c3ab7c concrete; the only near-white is the specular sun flare #fcfbfc); BR2049's brightest interiors are desaturated grey-gold; the overcast park scenes are a flat grey-green. A "light" or high-key moment is still a tinted, desaturated wash (warm gold or cold grey), never sRGB neutral. If the site ever needs a lighter state, tint and desaturate it — don't go to paper-white. (ref: 016-drive-neon; src: https://film-grab.com/2012/07/01/drive/)

13. [WORKING] **The grade lives in the air — implement it partly as atmospheric haze in the base hue.** Vegas dust (010), the amber interior light-shafts (012), the seawall mist (013), and the noodle-bar steam (019) all carry the color volumetrically: it thickens with distance and hazes the far field, not just tinting surfaces. Add the grade partly as an atmospheric fog/vignette in the base hue that deepens toward the edges and distance, so color reads as a medium the scene sits in rather than a flat LUT on layers. (Cross-ref rain P7 atmospheric perspective.) (ref: 010-vegas-dust, 013-seawall; src: https://film-grab.com/2019/05/24/blade-runner-2049/)

14. [WORKING] **Synthesis for the daikieOS grade.** Base at the value floor (P1, ≈#0a1416 teal-black) → one temperature per section, switched at boundaries (P3) → the cool base is a single stable teal-cyan token reused everywhere (P9) → warm accents (amber ≈#c37a2a, signal red) enter only as motivated point emitters at ~1–6% of frame with a bloomed core+halo (P2, P5, P7) → subjects/HUD separate by warm-on-cool + luminance + same-family glow, not complementary blocks (P8, P11) → green is a rare "living/other" third accent (P10) → the whole sits inside an atmospheric haze of the base hue (P13). The site's existing cyan holo-shell + amber city-emitter split (globe P11) is period-accurate to this language; the code already carries the right tokens (`css/site.css`: `--cyan #39f0ff`, `--amber #ff9e2c`, `--cyan-dim #1c6f7a`, `--amber-dim #7a4d14`, and `--bloom-cyan`/`--bloom-amber` additive glows — so P5/P11 same-hue bloom is partly built). The concrete color elevation targets (bearing, no code here): (a) **crush the base further to the floor** (P1) — the current base literals `#05060a`/`#030407` are already near-black but read blue-black, not the film's *teal*-black; (b) **split the base from the accent** (P9) — `--cyan #39f0ff` is a bright electric *emitter*, so introduce a separate desaturated dark-teal *base* token (≈ hue 185–195°, e.g. #0c1f22) rather than tinting the base with the emitter cyan; (c) **enforce the accent-area budget** (P2); and (d) **let bloom + atmospheric fog carry the color instead of raising midtones** (P5, P13). (ref: 010-vegas-dust, 002-lapd, 013-seawall)

## Thin areas / open questions
- **Drive's magenta/cyan neon look is under-sampled.** Frame-position guessing on film-grab landed mostly on Drive's *daylight* scenes (015 night-skyline and 018 tunnel are the only true noir Drive frames kept); the iconic pink-elevator and cyan night-drive frames were not captured. The magenta-neon *hue* is covered by BR2049's Joi ad (009), but a dedicated Drive/neon-noir grade pass (or CP2077 key art) would broaden the cross-film base beyond the two Blade Runners.
- **[DATA] is measured but not colorimetric.** Hexes and ratios come from PIL on downscaled, JPEG-compressed, already-graded film-grab frames (not RAW, not a calibrated pipeline) — they faithfully describe *these frames* but are not scene-referred values; treat them as reliable relative structure (value %, area ratios, hue family), not absolute cinematography spec. A calibrated grab would sharpen the exact tokens.
- **artstation.com color scripts uncaptured** (page HTML 403 Cloudflare); the study leans entirely on live-action film grades, so hand-painted color-script *keys* (fewer, bolder color blocks) are a thin area — an og:image/CDN fallback capture could add the illustrator's-eye reduction.
- **moviesincolor swatch reductions replaced, not captured** — I generated my own PIL palette reductions rather than using the site's; that is arguably stronger but means there is no third-party "authoritative" palette cross-check on file. The premiumbeat article (P10) is the only external corroboration of a specific color *intent*.
- **Neon-bloom falloff and grade-transition timing are spatial/temporal unknowns here.** Bloom radius vs. core brightness, and how fast a scene switches temperature at a cut, are not measured (single stills); the bloom is [EXTRACTED] shape only, and transition cadence belongs to the motion surface.
